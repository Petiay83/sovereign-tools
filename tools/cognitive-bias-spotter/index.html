📖 The Science of Cognitive Restructuring

1. Thoughts Are Not Facts

The brain is a predictive survival engine, not a truth-telling organ. Under social threat, evolutionary psychology biases us toward hyper-vigilance: assuming hostility, rejection, or catastrophe to avoid surprise danger.

2. The Amygdala Hijack

When an automatic thought triggers fear or shame, the amygdala floods the bloodstream with cortisol and adrenaline within 100 milliseconds, shutting down nuanced prefrontal reasoning. Naming the distortion halts this cascade.

3. Not Toxic Positivity

A rational reframe is NOT "Everything is wonderful!" It is ruthless, sober objectivity: acknowledging genuine challenges while stripping away the catastrophizing fiction created by your anxiety.
