📜 Foundational Stoic Precepts

1. The Enchiridion (Epictetus)

"Make it your study then to confront every harsh impression with the words: 'You are but an appearance, and not at all the thing you represent.' And examine it: is it concerning things in our power, or not? If not, have ready the answer: 'It is nothing to me.'"

2. Internalizing Goals (William B. Irvine)

"When playing tennis, setting the goal of winning will bring disappointment if you lose. But setting the goal of playing your best possible game guarantees peace of mind regardless of the score."

3. The Citadel Within (Marcus Aurelius)

"You have power over your mind — not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength. The mind that is free from passion is a citadel, for man has nothing more secure to which he can fly for refuge."
