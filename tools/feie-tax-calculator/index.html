⏰ The 24-Hour Midnight Rule

Under IRS Publication 54, a "full day" is strictly a 24-hour period beginning at midnight and ending at midnight. The day you leave the United States or arrive in a foreign country generally does NOT count as a foreign day because you were not present for the entire 24 hours.

🔄 Arbitrary 12-Month Period

Your 12-month qualifying period does not need to match the calendar year (Jan 1 to Dec 31). It can begin on any day of the year (e.g., May 14 to May 13 of the following year). You may choose whatever window maximizes your tax deduction.

🏠 Tax Home Requirement

In addition to the physical day count, IRS Section 911 requires maintaining your "tax home" in a foreign country. You cannot maintain an "abode" in the United States while claiming FEIE, meaning your regular place of business or economic ties must be foreign.
