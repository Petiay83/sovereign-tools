📐 The Mathematical Mechanics of Geo-Arbitrage

1. The Pre-Tax Advantage

Reducing your burn by $2,500/month by moving from NYC to Chiang Mai is mathematically superior to earning an extra $3,500/month in salary. Salary is taxed at 30–45% marginal rates, whereas living cost reductions represent pure, untaxed free cash flow.

2. The Compounding Buffer

In a high-burn city, capital drops so fast that compounding interest has no time to act. In low-burn hubs, money market yield (4–5%) offsets a substantial fraction of your total living expenses, stretching your timeline asymptotically.

3. The Crossover Threshold

Once your monthly side/freelance income plus investment yield equals local living costs, your runway becomes infinite. Achieving this in London requires ~$4,500/mo net; in Da Nang or Bansko, it requires under ~$1,000/mo.
