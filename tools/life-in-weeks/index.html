📜 Seneca & The Calculus of Time

1. Life Is Not Short

"It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it. Life is long if you know how to use it."

— Seneca, De Brevitate Vitae (On the Shortness of Life)

2. The Tail End Reality

By the time you graduate high school, you have already spent roughly 93% of the total in-person face time you will ever have with your parents. If you see them 3 times a year for a few days, you are living in the final 5% of the relationship. Make each visit intentional.

3. The Power of Finite Counting

When something feels infinite, you treat it casually. When you realize you only have 40 more summers or 300 more books to read, choice becomes urgent. You stop reading books you hate, stop spending evenings with people who drain you, and start living deliberately.
