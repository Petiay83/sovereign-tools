🛡️ Actionable Evidence-Based Clinical Levers

🥗 Dietary Interventions

  • Reduce Saturated Fat: Saturated fatty acids downregulate LDL receptor (LDLR) recycling on hepatocytes, spiking ApoB.
  • Viscous Soluble Fiber: 10–15g/day psyllium or beta-glucan binds bile acids, forcing the liver to clear ApoB particles.
  • Lower Glycemic Load: High refined carbs and fructose drive hepatic de novo lipogenesis, elevating VLDL/Triglycerides.

🏃 Exercise & Zone 2 Training

  • Mitochondrial Fat Oxidation: 150–180 min/week of Zone 2 cardio enhances muscle triglyceride clearance and boosts lipoprotein lipase (LPL).
  • Resistance Training: Increases GLUT4 translocation and reverses peripheral insulin resistance, rapidly crushing high TG/HDL ratios.
  • Visceral Fat Reduction: Direct reduction in portal free fatty acids entering the liver.

💊 Pharmacotherapy (Clinical Context)

  • Statins (HMG-CoA Reductase): Upregulates hepatic LDL receptors; decreases ApoB by 30–50%.
  • Ezetimibe (NPC1L1 Inhibitor): Blocks intestinal cholesterol absorption by ~50%; adds an extra 15–20% ApoB reduction.
  • PCSK9 Inhibitors / siRNA: Prevents LDLR degradation, dropping ApoB to physiological infant levels (< 30 mg/dL).
