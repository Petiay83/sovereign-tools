📖 The Philosophical Mechanics of Rosenberg's NVC

1. The Video Camera Test

"You are messy" is a moralistic judgment. "Your jacket has been on the dining chair for three days" is an observation. When we mix evaluation with observation, people hear criticism and resist everything that follows.

2. The Pseudo-Feeling Trap

Saying "I feel ignored" is not an emotion; it is an accusation: "I think you are deliberately neglecting me." Genuine feelings (lonely, anxious, sad) originate inside you and describe your body's state without indicting the other person.

3. Request vs Demand

How do you know if your request was secretly a demand? By how you treat the person if they say "no". If a "no" results in guilt-tripping, cold silence, or anger, it was a coercive demand, not a genuine request.
