📜 The Foundations of Axiological Compatibility

1. Why Hobbies Don't Matter

Couples rarely split because one likes hiking and the other likes cinema. Partnerships dissolve over structural friction: whether to risk capital on an asymmetric venture, whether to raise children, and how to speak the truth when tension peaks.

2. Complementarity vs Conflict

Some polarities are healthy complements: an analytical partner paired with an intuitive partner can form an indomitable team. Other polarities are zero-sum dealbreakers: wanting borderless nomadism while the other demands rooted homeownership leads to irreconcilable resentment.

3. The Power of Explicit Vectors

Most people discover their partner's stance on financial risk or childrearing five years too late. Laying down your 12-dimensional axiological vector turns implicit, simmering landmines into clear, conscious design choices.
